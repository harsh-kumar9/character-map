What the landscape shows. Three things, in order of size.

  • The subspace is organized by utterance type, not by honesty. In panels a and b the first component (71% of the subspace variance at layer 17, 46% at layer 29) separates Gemma's own replies about someone else (recognize, judge) from replies written in Gemma's voice (act, self-describe); the second separates act from self-describe. Inside each cluster the four definitions overlap, and the formality control's items sit in the same places. Where an item lands says what kind of utterance it is, not which honesty it has or lacks.
  • Polarity is a small offset inside those clusters, with two exceptions. The centred views (menu in the rotatable figure) remove each unit's mean. Self-knowledge has by far the largest + to − separation (15 standardized units in the act frame and 15 in self-description at layer 17), and its two arrows point the same way: "I don't know the answer" is, in form, a statement about oneself, which is why the act and self-describe directions for this definition converge with depth and why it entangles with self-deprecation. Sincerity in the own-reply frames is the other large one (judge 6, recognize 3), with its two arrows parallel and orthogonal to self-knowledge's.
  • The third component is the self-knowledge axis in self-description. At layer 17 the first two components are utterance type and self-knowledge-in-action; the third (6% of the subspace variance) is where "I know the answer" separates from "I do not" in self-description (a + to − gap of 16 units on that axis, against 2 or less for every conduct contrast), so the two self-knowledge arrows span a plane of their own. At layer 29 the third component (11%) also carries the sincerity judgment contrast. Rotating the 3D view shows the clusters as three plates, own reply, act and self-describe, with the self-knowledge arrows crossing between the last two.
  • Honesty in conduct is a small, consistent shift. Sincerity, accuracy and honesty-humility in the act and self-describe frames all have separations of 0.3 to 2.3 units, the size of the formality control's (1.7). Their directions are real (within-frame AUROC up to 0.97 for honesty-humility), but a matched honest and dishonest reply differ by a few words, and that difference is small next to the axes that encode who is speaking and in what form. There is no honesty region in this model, and no honesty manifold of the kind one sees for position or rhyme: there are utterance-type clusters, and inside them small offsets that point in different directions for different definitions.

Two consequences for the design. Any probe or steering vector for "honesty" built from own-reply judgments will mostly encode the own-reply format unless that axis is projected out, which is what the verdict control does. And an evaluation that reads the self-describe frame is reading a cluster that honesty-humility and sincerity share with the formality control; what distinguishes "I am honest" from "I am formal" is a small offset inside it.
